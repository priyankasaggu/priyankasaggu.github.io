A small post, to celebrate and document a personal milestone. :)

I am elected to the [Kubernetes Steering Committee](https://github.com/kubernetes/community/blob/main/elections/steering/2026/results.md) in the 2026 elections.

I am extremely grateful for the incredible amount of support and encouragement, I received from the Kubernetes community and my friends during both the cycles I ran in.
(I ran in the last 2 cycles. My nominations from both times: [2025](https://github.com/kubernetes/community/issues/8580) and [2026](https://github.com/kubernetes/community/issues/9092).)

Both times, I learned an enormous amount.  
Both times, I received a lot of community support, for which I am extremely grateful  
(to **_every single person_** who took the time and interest to vote.)  
I couldn't have done it alone. It took the trust and support of the community.  
(In fact, during the last election cycle too, I received net positive winning votes, and I lost a seat by a single vote.)

Right now is just the beginning of a new role for me.  
I am going to take some time to learn the behind-the-scenes of steering (while I continue everything else on my plate.)  
I look forward to using this opportunity to make a meaningful impact. :)
